About

Dr Qinbiao (Leo) Li is a Research Assistant Professor in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He received his BEng (2017) and MEng (2020) from Shandong University, and his PhD (2024) from PolyU, with a research attachment at Nanyang Technological University. He then served as a Postdoctoral Fellow at PolyU before taking up his current position.

His research sits at the intersection of human factors, neuro-ergonomics, transport engineering, and artificial intelligence for intelligent transportation (aviation, driving, UAM) safety. Dr Li is Principal Investigator (PI) on projects funded by the Hong Kong Smart Traffic Fund and the National Natural Science Foundation of China, and has served as Guest Editor for Aerospace and IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, and as a member of the Editorial Board of Humanities and Social Sciences Communications.

Research Areas

Theoretical and computational studies on human-centric intelligence for safe and adaptive autonomous systems in transportation, integrating neuro-ergonomics, cognitive science, human factors engineering, and artificial intelligence.

  • Human-centric intelligent transportation systems (driving, aviation and eVTOL).
  • Human-autonomy teaming and co-adaptive collaboration.
  • Predictive-prescriptive transportation safety and proactive intervention.
  • Neuro-ergonomics and human cognitive modelling.
  • Neural networks and AI-driven applications.
  • Human/Brain-computer interaction.
  • Pattern analysis on situational awareness, fatigue, stress, and cognitive load.
  • Human factors engineering and experiment-oriented ergonomics in transportation.
  • Industrial design, interaction, and performance assessment.
